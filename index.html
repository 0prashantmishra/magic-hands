How It Works

From your box, to a wall piece

A real sequence — five steps, every order, in this order.

01

You send the item

Courier your garland, curls or keepsake, or drop it off locally.For baby casting we visit the location with prior fixed schedule.

02

We plan the layout

I share a layout preview and confirm size, frame style and finish with you.

03

Dry & arrange

Florals are dried and pressed; the design is arranged before any resin touches it.

04

Pour & cure

Resin is poured in layers and cured over several days, fully by hand.

05

Polish & ship

Edges are sanded, the piece is polished, packed securely and shipped or handed over to you.
